Privacy information

Last updated November 2026 · Pre-release beta information. Check this page against the specific app version you use. CyberShield is an independent cyber-safety education project, not a government service or certified antivirus.

Information you provide

Local message, link, phone scam, UPI text and chosen-file assessments are processed on your device when possible. Selected Android QR images are decoded locally. A result does not prove that something is safe. CyberShield does not automatically read private messaging apps, contacts, call recordings or the contents of other installed apps.

Online checks and AI

If you choose an online reputation check, a URL, domain, phone number or file hash may be sent through the CyberShield HTTPS backend to authorized providers. A DNS query may send a domain name to Google Public DNS. If you consent to use online AI, your submitted question is processed by the configured third-party AI provider. Do not provide OTPs, passwords or other secrets. Third-party privacy and retention practices apply. Selected screenshot OCR uploads an image only when you separately consent to a supported server-side OCR option.

Android and iOS permissions

Android package-inspection results show reported permissions of visible apps, not their actual camera, microphone or screen-sharing activity. Sideloaded Android versions may ask for optional overlay and APK installation permissions. Installation always requires system approval. Google Play versions use Play updates and request fewer permissions. iOS cannot inspect other apps or their private permission histories.

Private administration

The protected admin dashboard authenticates authorized operators through GitHub. It stores operator account IDs, approved public editorial settings and audit logs in a restricted database; it does not provide access to users' private phone contents. Admin passwords or management tools are not bundled with apps.

Optional anonymous analytics

Anonymous usage statistics are off by default and require an explicit opt-in in supported web and Android versions. After consent, a temporary random session ID, product surface, activity heartbeat and download-click event may be sent to CyberShield. The server stores a one-way digest and coarse timing/counters for up to approximately 14 days. It does not place phone numbers, messages, QR data, contacts, checked URLs or raw IP addresses into the analytics database. The dashboard shows approximate consenting sessions, not all active users or installed devices. Public GitHub APK downloads do not represent confirmed installations. You can withdraw consent in the app.

Hosting, logs and security

Hosting and API providers may process normal network metadata, including request times and IP addresses, and third-party providers may retain requests under their terms. CyberShield uses HTTPS where supported and does not claim 100% protection. Privacy declarations and retention practices are subject to review before store launch.

Questions and reporting

For questions about the project, contact the developer using the official website or developer profile. Report active Indian cybercrime through cybercrime.gov.in; for immediate financial loss contact your bank and call 1930.
