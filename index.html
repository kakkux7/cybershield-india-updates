Responsible protection

Security should be understandable.

CyberShield is designed to make suspicious behavior easier to recognize without hiding the uncertainty behind it.

01 Results explain what was found and why it matters.
02 Selected local checks don't automatically upload personal files or messages.
03 Remote provider checks require a configured backend and relevant consent.
04 Android installation and permission decisions stay with you.
Important limits

No false promise of “100% safe.”

This is an experimental beta, not a certified antivirus. It cannot rule out spyware, inspect every private app file, determine who owns every phone number, confirm WhatsApp registration, or guarantee a website is harmless. A missed threat-list match does not mean a safe link.

For active financial cyber fraud in India, immediately call 1930, contact your bank and report through the official cybercrime.gov.in portal.

Independent project. Not an official Government of India app.
