TOOL 03 / MANUAL APP REVIEW

Your apps. Your control.

Select a currently installed app to review its permissions, installation source and signing certificate. These checks are useful clues, not proof of malware or spying.

▣ Review installed apps and permissions

Android beta: inspect locally visible installed applications, including permissions that have been requested/granted. See which apps have camera or microphone access, and open their Android settings. This does NOT detect active spying or prove malware.

Screen recording is controlled by separate Android consent and indicators. Actual past camera/microphone use is shown by Android Privacy Dashboard, not inferred from permissions.

01

Stop and verify

Do not click suspicious links, download APKs, reveal OTPs or enter a UPI PIN to receive money. Contact the organization independently.

02

If money was lost

Call India’s cybercrime helpline 1930 promptly and report at the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal. Contact your bank immediately.

03

If an account is compromised

Change the password through the official app/site, sign out other sessions, turn on two-factor authentication and alert your bank if relevant.

04

If you installed a suspicious app

Disconnect from sensitive accounts, uninstall the app if possible, review permissions and seek reputable device-security help. Consider a reset if compromise is confirmed.
